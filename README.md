## 📖 The Story

In this rhythm-based journey, you take on the mantle of 

**Charon**, the ferryman of the Underworld. But this time, your oar is not your only tool. To guide the deceased, you must master the strings of a celestial **Lyre**.

Every soul that boards your boat carries the weight of their past—pains, arrogance, or deep laments—that threaten to make them fade away before they can reach peace. It is your duty to play the correct melodies to soothe these souls, ensuring they survive the transit to the **Elysian Fields**.

Throughout the voyage, a mysterious **Raven** accompanies you, questioning your purpose and reminding you of the ultimate temptation: crossing the very border you guard to find your own eternal rest.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Features

* **Rhythmic Soul-Sooting:** Use musical notes to stabilize fading souls.
* **Three Progressive Stages:**
    1.  **Tartarus:** The depths of torment.
    2.  **Valley of Souls:** The misty path of reflection.
    3.  **Elysian Fields:** The final destination of peace.
* **Special Ability:** Fill your rhythm meter to unleash a powerful melody that boosts your score.
